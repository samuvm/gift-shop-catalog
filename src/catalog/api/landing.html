Find the right gift in a minute

Tell our gift assistant who it's for and what you'd like to spend. It looks through the shop's catalogue, with live prices, stock and delivery times, and suggests up to three gifts with a reason for each.

Try asking

How it works

  1. Tell it who and how much

    Who the gift is for, the occasion and your budget, in your own words.

  2. It asks at most one question

    Only if something important is missing, such as the budget.

  3. Up to three picks, each with a reason

    Real products from the catalogue, with price, stock and delivery time.

Ribbon & Co. is a demo shop built for a technical assignment. The products come from a sample catalogue and nothing here is for sale.

For developers: the catalogue API is described at /openapi.json, and the same tools are served over MCP (Streamable HTTP) at /mcp. Both need an API key.
